## [Terry Tao: Math Must Value More Than Proof as AI Automates It](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/09/18/if-math-is-more-than-proof-we-need-to-better-celebrate-the-rest-of-it/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Terry Tao published an essay on September 18, 2026 arguing that mathematics should better celebrate and value activities beyond formal proof, including intuition, computation, and communication. He frames this as increasingly urgent because AI systems are beginning to automate proof-based work. Tao is one of the most influential living mathematicians, so his argument carries weight in debates about research culture and how mathematical work is judged and rewarded. If proof is increasingly automated, the criteria for tenure, funding, and recognition may need to shift toward intuition, exposition, computation, and problem formulation. The essay builds on Tao's earlier career-advice post 'There's more to mathematics than rigour and proofs,' which stressed intuition, heuristics, and plausible reasoning as paths to rigorous results. Tao has also become a prominent advocate for AI in mathematics, where automated proof-checkers let problems be split into small, verifiable chunks.

hackernews · num42 · Sep 19, 06:28 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49763928)

**Background**: Mathematics has traditionally been organized around the production of rigorous proofs, a standard associated with Hilbert's program and the formalist tradition that followed the 1900 Poincaré–Hilbert debate. Automated theorem proving is a long-standing subfield of computer science, and recent AI systems, including LLMs, have made significant progress on proving and checking mathematical results. This raises the question of what human mathematicians should focus on if proof itself becomes largely machine-assisted.
