## [Mistral releases open weights for 1T-parameter Mistral Large 4](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4-oss/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Mistral has released the open weights of Mistral Large 4 (nicknamed 'Le Chonk'), a 1-trillion-parameter multimodal Mixture-of-Experts model, alongside a significant price cut for its API. The new pricing is nearly half the preview rate: $0.7 per million input tokens and $2.1 per million output tokens, compared to $1.36 and $4.18 previously. Releasing weights for a frontier-scale 1T-parameter model is a major milestone for open-source AI, as it allows researchers and companies to inspect, fine-tune, and deploy the model on their own infrastructure. The steep price cut also makes Mistral more competitive against other API providers, potentially benefiting cost-sensitive enterprises and EU-based users seeking local alternatives. Mistral Large 4 uses a granular Mixture-of-Experts design that activates 52B of 1.05T total parameters, with a 1.6B vision encoder for image input, and offers three reasoning modes for cost-performance trade-offs. It leads open-weight benchmarks in coding and defensive security, achieving a 96.6% average refusal rate on malicious cyber requests, though community members note the lack of data on non-malicious request handling.

hackernews · kuberwastaken · Oct 29, 14:13 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50289418)

**Background**: Open weights are the publicly released learned parameters of a trained AI model, allowing anyone to download and run the model. A 1-trillion-parameter model is at the frontier of scale, typically requiring cloud infrastructure to serve, and Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures activate only a subset of parameters per input to reduce inference costs. Mistral, a French AI company, has been expanding its offerings with third-party models and features like the Responses API, positioning itself as an EU-friendly provider.

## [Van der Waerden number w(2;3,21)=416 proved with Lean 4](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1xivpue/w2321_416_a_van_der_waerden_number_conjectured_by/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

The van der Waerden number w(2;3,21)=416, conjectured by Kullmann in 2010 and still listed as open by DeepMind's Formal Conjectures, has now been proved with a Lean 4 kernel-checked proof. The proof was generated by LPM, a neuro-symbolic system from Hupyy, and covers 209 cases with 38,661,140 leaf formulas, each refuted by the verified LRAT checker cake_pr. This resolves a long-standing open combinatorial conjecture with a fully machine-checked proof, marking a significant milestone for both formal mathematics and AI-assisted theorem proving. It demonstrates that neuro-symbolic systems can produce proofs that are not only correct but also verifiable by trusted kernels, potentially accelerating the formalization of open problems. The proof is a Lean 4 theorem over 209 cases and 38,661,140 leaf formulas, each refutation checked by cake_pr (the LRAT checker verified in CakeML) and bound by digest to its Lean term. A 13.6 GB evidence package including Lean sources, checker records, and a replay kit is available, and nothing was counted unless the Lean kernel or cake_pr accepted it.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/Rodriguez909 · Oct 29, 20:46

**Background**: Van der Waerden's theorem states that for any positive integers r and k, there exists a smallest N such that any r-coloring of {1,...,N} contains a monochromatic arithmetic progression of length k; this N is the van der Waerden number W(r,k). Lean 4 is a proof assistant and functional programming language based on dependent type theory, used to formalize and verify mathematical proofs. LRAT is a proof format for SAT solvers, and cake_pr is a verified LRAT checker implemented in CakeML, a formally verified compiler and runtime system.

## [Binance Hack: $5.6B Stolen, $2.4B Frozen](https://arkm.com/explorer/entity/21a94755-f604-45e5-9501-6542e23005e8) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A hacker allegedly stole $5.6 billion from Binance, the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange, with approximately $2.4 billion already frozen by stablecoin issuers Circle and Tether. Binance confirmed it is investigating the incident, which its founder said "does look like a hack on Binance." If confirmed, this would be the largest cryptocurrency hack ever, potentially exceeding the Mt. Gox collapse in scale and shaking confidence in centralized exchanges. The rapid freezing of stablecoins highlights the growing power of issuers like Circle and Tether to intervene in on-chain assets, raising questions about centralization in crypto. The frozen funds include 971.15M USDT and 823M USDC on Ethereum, 312.73M USDT on Tron, and 175M USDT on Ethereum, according to on-chain data. The incident sparked extensive discussion on Hacker News with over 1,000 upvotes and 600 comments, and notable figures like Molly White have suggested the reports are credible.

hackernews · Tiberium · Oct 29, 21:38 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50296470)

**Background**: Stablecoins like USDT (Tether) and USDC (Circle) are cryptocurrencies pegged to the US dollar, widely used for trading and settlements. Their issuers can freeze funds on-chain, a feature that has been used before to recover stolen assets but also raises concerns about control. Binance is the world's largest crypto exchange by trading volume, making any security breach highly consequential for the entire market.
