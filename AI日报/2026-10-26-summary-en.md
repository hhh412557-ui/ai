## [OpenAI and Anthropic Slash LLM Prices by Up to 80%](https://minimaxir.com/2026/10/cheap-llms/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

OpenAI and Anthropic have cut their large language model API prices by up to 80% within a few months, according to a new analysis. The price reductions reflect intensifying competition and falling inference costs. The steep price cuts signal a shift in the LLM market from premium pricing to commodity competition, affecting developers, enterprises, and the business models of AI labs. It also highlights pressure from open-source and Chinese models like DeepSeek and Kimi. The analysis attributes the cuts to competition, inference cost economics, and moat-building strategies such as enterprise commitments and regulatory capture. DeepSeek's reported $38 revenue per GPU per hour suggests significant room for further price reductions.

hackernews · minimaxir · Oct 25, 21:11 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50236102)

**Background**: Inference cost is the per-token price of running a trained LLM to generate outputs, and it dominates the economics of production AI applications at scale. Token pricing has compressed over 90% since 2022, while consumer subscription prices have remained largely unchanged. Open-source models from Chinese labs like DeepSeek and Qwen have gained traction, intensifying price competition.
