## [Simon Willison's Annotated Keynote Recaps 2026 in LLMs](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/27/2026-in-llms-so-far/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On September 25, 2026, Simon Willison delivered the closing keynote at the WeAreDevelopers World Congress North America in San Jose, and on September 27 he published the video, slides, and a detailed annotated transcript titled "2026 in LLMs (so far)." The talk traces the year chronologically, starting from what he calls the November 2025 inflection point marked by the releases of Claude Opus 4.5 and GPT-5.1. Willison is one of the most widely followed independent voices in the LLM developer community, so his chronological synthesis serves as a useful reference for tracking how the year's model releases and coding-agent advances fit together. It is not a breakthrough itself, but a high-value retrospective that helps developers and observers understand the trajectory of the field. The keynote highlights that Claude Opus 4.5 and GPT-5.1 were incremental model improvements, but when paired with their coding agent harnesses (Claude Code and Codex) they crossed a threshold from "often make mistakes" to "reliable enough to use on a day-to-day basis." Willison also continues his long-running "pelican riding a bicycle" SVG benchmark, noting that as of November the models still struggled to draw a coherent bicycle.
